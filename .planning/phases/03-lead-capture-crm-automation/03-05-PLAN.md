<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** TravelSentro Admin or Staff member, **I want to** have every customer inquiry automatically captured as a tracked CRM lead — with the customer instantly acknowledged and my team instantly notified — and be able to view, search, and manage those leads, **so that** no inquiry is ever lost and I can follow up efficiently.

(Same phase-level user story as 03-01/03-02/03-03/03-04-PLAN.md.)

## This Plan

Build the CRM contact detail page — where Admin/Staff actually work a lead: see its full inquiry history as a timeline (CRM-02), see which package(s) it's about (CRM-06), change its status (CRM-04), and edit its contact info (CRM-03), all with the exact permission-gated-rendering behavior 03-UI-SPEC.md specifies (Staff without `can_edit_crm` sees identical data, read-only, no edit affordances at all — not a hidden page, a rendered-differently one). This is the plan that makes CRM-07's audit trail (created_by_name/updated_by_name from 03-01's schema) actually visible.

Depends on 03-04 for two reasons: `lib/crm/status.ts` (the shared status label/badge contract, avoiding a duplicate 5-way mapping) and to avoid a same-wave `package.json` conflict — `date-fns` is installed once by 03-04 and reused here.

Purpose: Complete the CRM admin surface this phase's success criteria require — searchable/filterable list (03-04) plus a fully worked detail view (this plan) together satisfy CRM-02 through CRM-07 end-to-end.
Output: `/admin/crm/[id]` — a live contact detail page with timeline, permission-gated status editor, and an Edit Contact dialog.
</objective>

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-14 | Elevation of Privilege | updateStatus()/updateContact() Server Actions | high | mitigate | Both start with requirePermission("can_edit_crm") (throws before any Supabase call), and the RLS UPDATE policy on contacts (03-01) independently requires has_permission(auth.uid(), 'can_edit_crm') — double-gated exactly like AUTH-05's established Phase 2 pattern, so even a compromised/bypassed Server Action call is still rejected at the database layer |
