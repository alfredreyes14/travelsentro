<objective>
## Phase Goal

**As a** TravelSentro Admin or Staff member, **I want to** have every customer inquiry automatically captured as a tracked CRM lead — with the customer instantly acknowledged and my team instantly notified — and be able to view, search, and manage those leads, **so that** no inquiry is ever lost and I can follow up efficiently.

(Same phase-level user story as 03-01/03-02-PLAN.md.)

## This Plan

Extend the `app/api/inquiries/route.ts` Route Handler built in 03-02 with the two remaining `after()`-scheduled side effects: an auto-reply email to the customer (AUTO-01) and a bcc'd internal notification to every Admin/Staff with `can_message_customers=true` (AUTO-02, D-06/D-07). Both stay nested inside the same `is_new` gate 03-02 already established, so this plan adds zero new dedup logic — it reuses the existing idempotency signal.

**Deviation from `.claude/CLAUDE.md`'s Supporting Libraries table:** CLAUDE.md lists `@react-email/components` as the React Email package. 03-RESEARCH.md's Package Legitimacy Audit found this package deprecated (`npm view @react-email/components deprecated` confirms "Package no longer supported") — React Email v6 unified everything into the single `react-email` package. This plan installs `react-email` only and imports all components (`Html`, `Head`, `Body`, `Container`, `Text`, `Button`, etc.) from it directly, per 03-RESEARCH.md's explicit correction of CLAUDE.md's stale guidance.

Purpose: Close the loop on "no lead lost, and the business knows immediately" — the customer gets instant acknowledgement, staff get an instant alert, both within Resend's free-tier cap at this business's scale (D-07).
Output: Two working, on-brand transactional emails, sent as best-effort, non-blocking, redelivery-safe side effects of the inquiry-ingestion endpoint.
</objective>
