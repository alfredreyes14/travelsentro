## Phase Goal

The homepage gains a hero carousel and admin-editable content sections -- value props, a featured packages grid, customer testimonials, an embedded inquiry form, and conditional Brand Partners/Corporate Clients logo sections -- all within the existing locked brand system.

(Same phase-level goal as 06-01-PLAN.md.)

## This Plan

Build the remaining 3 presentational homepage components: Testimonials (the one section needing a new star-rating display and Avatar-with-fallback pattern) and the two independently-conditional logo band sections (Brand Partners, Corporate Clients). Like 06-03, all 3 are pure prop-driven components with zero direct Supabase calls -- 06-07 owns every query. This plan depends only on 06-01 (for the `avatar` component and `lucide-react`'s already-installed `StarIcon`) and runs in parallel with 06-02 and 06-03.

**Why Brand Partners and Corporate Clients are two separate files, not one parameterized component:** D-07 explicitly requires each section's visibility to be independently determined (never a combined "any partner exists" check). Two nearly-identical but genuinely separate components makes this independence structurally obvious at the call site in 06-07, rather than relying on a caller to correctly pass two separate boolean flags into one shared component.

Purpose: Give 06-07's homepage composition a ready-to-import Testimonials section and two independently-conditional partner/client logo bands.
Output: 3 new components under `components/homepage/`, all compiling cleanly and following UI-SPEC's Section Layout & Composition, Color, and Copywriting contracts exactly.
</objective>
